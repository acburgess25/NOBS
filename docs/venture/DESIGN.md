# NOBS Design Review + Brand

## Brand mark
- **`landing/assets/nobs-logo.svg`** — rounded-square gradient (violet→teal) with a
  neural "brain-of-synapses" core. The nodes/links read as a local brain; the small
  status dot is the "always-on local agent." Fits the *on-device, private, $0-marginal-cost*
  positioning.

## Design principles (recommended)
1. **Local & private = trust.** Dark UI, soft violet/teal accents, no "cloud-flashy" gradient noise.
   Communicate *"yours, on your hardware"* visually — this is the differentiator vs. SaaS.
2. **One calm focal point per screen.** Landing hero: logo + one line + one CTA (waitlist).
3. **Type:** system font stack (SF/Monospace off) for trust; a mono accent for the "terminal-ish"
   power-user edge on Pro/Business tiers.
4. **Motion:** subtle (pulsing status dot only). Avoid hero carousels — you convert on a single idea.
