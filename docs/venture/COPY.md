# NOBS — Copy Deck (v1)

Source for landing + launch. Voice: **calm, direct, privacy-first**. Trust-building over hype.

## H1 (hero headline)
> **Your AI manager. On your machine. It gets smarter.**
Why it works: three beats — it's *yours* (local), it *works* (manager), it *learns* (improves).

## Subhead
> NOBS is a private AI manager that plans your day, remembers insights, and
> invents better ways to work — all on your own Apple Silicon. No cloud, no
> per-token bills, no data leaving your laptop.

## Primary CTA
- **Join the waitlist** (email capture, hero + plans)
- Secondary: **See pricing**

## The 3 pains → features (from the landing page)
| Pain | Feature | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| "AI tools cost me per token / leak my data" | Local-first, on-device (MLX) | Runs on your GPU, $0 inference |
| "Assistants don't remember how I work" | Self-improving skills | Learns reusable skills from your runs |
| "I have ideas but no system to work them" | Venture brain | Scores ideas, recommends what to build |

## Objection handling
1. **"Is it as smart as ChatGPT?"** — It's an *operator*, not a chatbot: it plans, files, and executes on *your* data. It runs your model, so it's as smart as the brain you bring.
2. **"Do I need to be technical?"** — Setup is one script. After that it's an app on your Mac.
3. **"Where's my data?"** — On your machine. Full stop. That's the whole point.
