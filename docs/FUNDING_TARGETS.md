# NOBS Funding Targets

**Status:** Research shortlist; not an endorsement or record of contact  
**Updated:** July 2, 2026

This is a deliberately small first-pass list for NOBS. It prioritizes stage fit, thematic fit, and a credible route to a conversation. Terms, application status, partner interests, and eligibility must be re-verified immediately before outreach.

## Working assumptions

- NOBS will raise its first institutional round at the pre-seed stage.
- The company is or will be headquartered in the Midwest. This must be confirmed before approaching geography-restricted funds.
- The immediate product is the private daily-planning assistant described in [`FUNDING_STRATEGY.md`](FUNDING_STRATEGY.md), not NOBSbox hardware.
- The founder can participate in a temporary in-person program only if confirmed.
- Product and validation evidence will be added before broad institutional outreach.

## Qualification notes

### LongJump

**Fit:** High, conditional on Midwest headquarters. LongJump states that it invests $110K at the earliest stages and has a strong Chicago bias. Its published guidance says an idea alone is generally insufficient; an early or manual product is expected. That matches the proposed sequence: demonstrate the planning loop, then apply.

**NOBS angle:** A Chicago-built consumer AI company proving that private, useful agents can emerge outside the coasts.

**Do not send yet:** A vision-only deck centered on hardware, smart homes, or a long feature roadmap.

### Y Combinator

**Fit:** High-upside accelerator application, but selection risk is high. The Fall 2026 batch is scheduled for October–December in San Francisco. YC states that it invests upon acceptance and accepts late applications, though on-time applications receive a defined decision window.

**NOBS angle:** “AI assistants know how to answer questions. NOBS knows when a person's day is impossible and helps fix it privately.”

**Application proof package:** founder video, two-minute product demo, concrete weekly progress, exact user evidence, incorporation/cap-table facts, and direct answers about Apple dependence and competition.

### Betaworks AI Camp

**Fit:** Thematic fit may be the strongest on the list. Its H2 2026 Camp calls for AI-native systems, trusted human-agent outcomes, and AI that leaves the computer to interact with the physical world. It advertises up to $500K and an in-residence component in New York.

**NOBS angle:** A permissioned personal agent that understands time, context, and trust well enough to intervene in a real day without becoming surveillance software.

**Diligence item:** Re-check the complete economics. Program-level headline funding can combine multiple instruments or ownership components.

### Afore

**Fit:** Stage and check size fit a larger lead round. Afore says it invests at pre-seed before traction and focuses on early software companies.

**NOBS angle:** Strong product vision plus a founder who can ship a distinct Apple-native experience before building the wider platform.

**Main risk:** Without a compelling founder biography, this is premature. Afore's own application guidance emphasizes why the founders are best suited to solve the problem.

### M25

**Fit:** Geography and stage are explicit. M25 publishes a scorecard covering team, market, product, traction, growth, funding, and business plan.

**NOBS angle:** Midwest-headquartered software with a capital-efficient wedge and optional subscription expansion.

**Main risk:** Consumer focus is not obvious from the published thesis. Seek a partner or portfolio signal before prioritizing.

### Lightbank

**Fit:** Broad early-stage mandate and deep Chicago network. The firm states that it invests before Series A in disruptive technology companies.

**NOBS angle:** A large consumer platform opportunity anchored by a concrete daily workflow.

**Main risk:** Current public thesis is broad, so warm introduction and partner-level fit matter more than the firm logo.

### Everywhere Ventures

**Fit:** Round construction is compatible: the firm publishes a $250K first check and participation in $500K–$2M pre-seed rounds.

**NOBS angle:** Add a globally networked pre-seed participant after a lead or local first check emerges.

**Main risk:** NOBS is not an obvious match to the sectors highlighted in its current FAQ. Treat as a syndicate target, not the first call.

## Investor discovery criteria

Add a new target only when evidence supports all of the following:

1. Invests at pre-seed or before meaningful revenue.
2. Typical first check can participate in a $250K–$500K round.
3. Has a credible consumer, productivity, agentic AI, privacy, Apple ecosystem, or local-compute thesis.
4. Does not require a business model that conflicts with [`PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md`](PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md).
5. Has a named route: application, partner, portfolio founder, founder LP, or trusted mutual connection.
