## 08 — Risks

Each material risk should name the current mitigation and the financing milestone that reduces it.

## Access policy

1. Share the brief deck and demo first.
2. Share aggregated evidence and product material after a qualified meeting.
3. Share legal, cap-table, security, and detailed financial material during real diligence.
4. Use named access, expiration, and audit logs where practical.
5. Revoke access after the process when appropriate.
6. Maintain one authoritative version of each artifact.
