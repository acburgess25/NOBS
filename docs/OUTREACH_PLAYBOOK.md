# NOBS Fundraising Outreach Playbook

**Status:** Draft; no messages have been sent  
**Purpose:** Run a concentrated, evidence-led fundraising process without spam or manufactured urgency.

## Readiness gate

Do not begin the main investor wave until all six are true:

- a two-minute demo shows the real planning loop;
- at least five target users have completed a prototype session;
- the deck contains current evidence rather than placeholder traction;
- the raise amount and 12–18 month milestones come from a bottom-up budget;
- incorporation, cap table, and IP ownership are understood;
- the founder can explain why they are uniquely suited to build NOBS.

Accelerator deadlines may justify an earlier application, but the application must remain honest about what works today.

## Process design

### Wave 0 — Narrative testing

Hold 5–8 conversations with founders, operators, and advisors who are not priority investors. Test:

- whether the problem is understood in one sentence;
- whether the wedge feels urgent rather than merely pleasant;
- whether the local/Tank/cloud design is an advantage or a distraction;
- the first skeptical question;
- which proof would change the listener's mind.

Revise the narrative when the same objection appears three times.

### Wave 1 — Highest-fit targets

Approach 6–10 targets over one week. Include the strongest program deadlines and best warm introductions. Cluster meetings rather than spacing them across months.

### Wave 2 — Evidence update

Use new user behavior, product progress, or pricing evidence to approach the next 8–12 targets. Do not send a cosmetic “just checking in” bump.

### Wave 3 — Syndicate and close

Once a credible lead or first check emerges, approach investors whose published check size and network make them good participants. State allocation and timing accurately.

## Accelerator application core answers

### What does the company make?

NOBS is a private personal-intelligence layer for the Apple ecosystem. It combines approved calendar, task, message, and priority context to detect when a person's day is infeasible, create a realistic plan, and propose reversible fixes. It runs locally when possible, can use hardware the customer owns for heavier work, and uses optional cloud capacity only under visible user policy.

### What is different?

Existing assistants answer questions. NOBS continuously earns enough trust to coordinate a person's day. Its product advantage is the combination of a high-frequency planning workflow, Apple-native context, user-approved memory, and transparent routing across local, user-owned, and cloud compute.

### What works today?

An interactive SwiftUI conversation prototype and FastAPI service baseline exist. The current agenda experience uses sample data. The next product milestone is the real consented daily-planning loop; report its live status and user counts at submission time.

### Why will this be large?

Daily planning is the entry point to communication, commitments, memory, research, household context, and automation. If NOBS earns repeated use and trust in the daily plan, it can become the personal policy and interaction layer across devices and services. This is a thesis, not proof; support it with market and user evidence before submission.

## Follow-up after a meeting

Send within the same day:

> Thanks for the direct conversation. My takeaways were: [two specific points]. You asked for [diligence item], which is here: [link]. Since we spoke, [only include a real update]. Our next financing milestone is [milestone/date]. I will follow up when [specific evidence] is ready.

Do not add someone to an investor update list without permission.

## Monthly investor update

Subject: NOBS — [month] progress: [one concrete result]

1. **Headline:** one sentence describing the most important outcome.
2. **Product:** what shipped and what users can now do.
3. **Evidence:** interviews, trials, return use, pricing commitments, and trust failures.
4. **Learning:** one belief strengthened and one belief weakened.
5. **Financing:** amount committed, active diligence, and remaining allocation—only when accurate.
6. **Asks:** no more than two specific introductions, candidates, or expertise requests.

## Pipeline discipline

For every target, record:

- thesis source and verification date;
- named partner and why that person fits;
- warm path and strength of relationship;
- contact date and response;
- meeting notes and objections;
- requested diligence;
- next action with a date;
- pass reason;
- permission to receive updates.

## Ethics and product alignment

- Never imply a warm relationship that does not exist.
- Never manufacture a competing term sheet, deadline, customer, or commitment.
- Never include private participant data in outreach or the data room.
- Never claim Apple endorsement, production entitlement, or platform access without proof.
- Treat a financing pass as evidence about investor fit, timing, team, product, or market—not as a product verdict by itself.
- Decline capital that requires advertising, personal-data monetization, artificial lock-in, or other conflict with [`PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md`](PRODUCT_DECISIONS.md).
