# NOBS Investor Brief

**Working draft — claims requiring founder or user evidence are marked.**

## Your technology. Finally working for you.

NOBS is a private personal-intelligence layer for the Apple ecosystem. Its first job is simple and painful: turn a chaotic mix of calendar events, tasks, messages, and priorities into a realistic daily plan.

Most assistants are either generic chatbots or cloud services that ask users to trade personal context for convenience. NOBS is local-first. It uses the iPhone when it can, user-owned Tank hardware when available, and optional NOBScloud only when the user permits it. Every response identifies where it was processed and can explain what information it used.

## The wedge

An overwhelmed working adult opens NOBS and sees that the day is not merely busy—it is infeasible. NOBS identifies the conflict, protects the work that matters, proposes a revised plan, and offers reversible fixes. The user stays in control.

This planning loop is the first product proof. Communication workflows, household intelligence, private research, smart-home unification, community skills, and future NOBSbox hardware expand from the same trusted relationship.

## Why now

- Apple devices increasingly support useful on-device intelligence.
- Consumers are adopting AI while becoming more aware of privacy, control, and subscription fatigue.
- Existing calendars, task managers, assistants, and smart-home products remain fragmented.
- A policy-controlled local/Tank/cloud router can deliver stronger capability without making cloud processing the default.

The first two claims need sourced market evidence before publication; the latter two are the product thesis to test with users.

## Product status

NOBS currently has:

- an interactive universal iPhone/iPad SwiftUI prototype with an overloaded-day conflict, revised-plan proposal, approve/reject states, and a local privacy receipt;
- the selected warm cream-and-sage Human Companion design direction;
- a FastAPI service baseline and portable cross-platform development workflow;
- approved product, privacy, automation, and business-model decisions;
- a public in-development project site.

The current daily-planning loop uses sample data. It builds across the representative [Device Hub QA matrix](DEVICE_HUB_QA.md), but physical-device testing, real calendar/task inputs, and user validation are the next proof milestones.

## Business model

Useful private local assistance remains free. Revenue comes from optional capability:

- NOBScloud for stronger models, secure fallback, research, and higher automation capacity;
- advanced local or Tank features;
- hosted Tank service, family plans, and future business plans;
- revenue share from reviewed paid skills;
- future NOBSbox hardware.

NOBS does not sell personal data or attention.

## Defensibility thesis

The defensibility is not a model wrapper. It is the combination of:

- an Apple-native daily workflow that earns repeated use;
- user-approved memory and preferences;
- a transparent policy layer across local, user-owned, and cloud compute;
- trust infrastructure for permissions, automation, privacy receipts, and skill safety;
- eventual household and cross-device continuity.

Each element must be demonstrated through product behavior and retention; it is not yet a proven moat.

## The raise

NOBS is preparing an initial angel/pre-seed round to fund 12–18 months of focused product development and validation. The working planning envelope is **$250,000–$500,000**, subject to a bottom-up budget and founder-runway decisions.

The round is intended to produce:

- repeated-use evidence from the primary user segment;

- evidence-backed pricing and NOBScloud packaging;
