## Description

NOBS is a private, local-first personal assistant for iPhone. It helps overwhelmed adults turn a chaotic day into a realistic plan—without selling your attention or requiring a cloud subscription.

**What works in this beta**
- Conversational onboarding that learns your name, load sources, and how much detail you want
- Morning briefing built from your Calendar and Reminders (only after you approve access)
- Evening wrap-up with an honest snapshot of the day
- Chat with visible **Local** or **Tank** processing labels and privacy receipts
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widget for today's plan
- Siri shortcuts: Prepare my day, Explain schedule, Ask NOBS
- Optional pairing with your own **Tank** server on your home network

**Optional Tank**
Tank is a self-hosted server you control. NOBS works locally without it. When Tank is available, briefings and chat can refine on your hardware—you always see where processing happened.

**Coming soon (honest boundaries)**
- Long-term Memory approval workflow
- Unified smart-home control
- Google Home and Alexa unification
- NOBScloud optional subscription (in-app products may appear but cloud capacity is not required)

**Privacy**
- No ads. No sale of personal data.
- Passwords and financial accounts are off-limits.
- Permissions are requested in context, not in a wall of switches.
