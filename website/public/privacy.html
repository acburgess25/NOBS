NOBS Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

NOBS is built by Alexander Burgess. This policy describes how the NOBS iPhone app handles information. NOBS does not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

What stays on your devices

  • Your name and preferences from onboarding
  • Conversation and briefing content processed on your iPhone
  • Calendar and Reminder data you permit for planning
  • Tank connection settings in the iOS Keychain

Optional Tank server

If you connect to Tank (a server you control), chat and briefing may be sent to that server on your network.

What we do not collect by default

  • No ad tracking
  • No sale of personal information
  • No passwords or financial account access
